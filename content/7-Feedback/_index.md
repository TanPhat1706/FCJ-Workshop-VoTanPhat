### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The working environment at the AWS office is incredibly comfortable and professional. The facilities, from the rooms and desks to hygiene and overall order, are maintained at a high standard. Beyond working hours, the FCAJ mentors and admins are extremely welcoming and sociable; they frequently took us out for lunch to discover great local food spots. This was a massive plus for team bonding.

**2. Support from Mentor / Team Admin**  
I am truly impressed by the enthusiasm of the team. Whenever we needed help with technical knowledge or clarification on rules, they were always ready to assist. They proactively communicated new regulations and gently reminded us of existing ones so no one would fall behind. Professionally, they were highly dedicated, providing in-depth feedback and rigorous evaluations on our teams' architectural designs. 

Alongside their support, they were also appropriately strict, enforcing rules fairly. Violations such as doing personal work, sleeping during working hours, or unexcused absences were handled firmly, maintaining a disciplined environment. I especially appreciated how they encouraged teams to step out of their comfort zones by registering as speakers at meetups to share and learn, and how they organized recreational activities like the Cloud Challenge to help us de-stress.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
This program has opened up an entirely new career path for me. Previously, I considered myself a pure software developer. However, after learning and gaining hands-on experience here, I have mastered core concepts of cloud computing and infrastructure. I now feel fully confident in pursuing a career in DevOps.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
I am deeply grateful for the high-quality events organized during the program. The guest speakers were dedicated experts who shared invaluable, battle-tested experiences. Beyond technical knowledge, my soft skills—such as presenting problems, defending architectural choices, and professional communication—have significantly improved.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
Regarding my team, I feel very lucky and happy to have worked with such responsible and energetic individuals. Everyone was eager to share knowledge, constantly exploring new technologies, and striving to improve themselves after every feedback session.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
Although the internship does not offer a financial allowance, it compensates with the immense flexibility of allowing us to proactively schedule our working hours. This made it very easy to balance my university studies with the internship.

### Additional Questions

**What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
The most satisfying aspect for me was gaining deep, hands-on exposure to cloud computing, specifically the AWS ecosystem. This is a critically important domain and serves as a solid foundation for my DevOps career path.

**What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
I would like to suggest setting up a water refill station in the office. Currently, it is quite inconvenient to carry 2-3 personal water bottles every day, and going out to convenience stores on the main street takes up a considerable amount of time.

**If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why or why not?**  
I completely support this and would highly recommend it to my friends. It is a rare opportunity to expand one's technical knowledge, experience a highly professional international working environment, and significantly improve communication and teamwork skills.

### Suggestions & Expectations

**Do you have any suggestions to improve the internship experience?**  
Aside from the water refill station mentioned above, I believe the current program is exceptionally well-designed.

**Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
The past 3 months have been a truly meaningful journey, and I have absorbed a massive amount of knowledge. While I deeply cherish my time here, I believe it is time for me to step out of my comfort zone and seek "new horizons" with tougher challenges to further push my limits. Thank you, FCAJ, for providing such an amazing launchpad!
