### Week 4 Objectives:

* Master advanced cost optimization strategies and comprehensive AWS billing management.
* Enhance infrastructure security posture by implementing advanced IAM policies, compliance hubs, and network firewalls.
* Automate data lifecycle management and integrate AWS development tools directly into local IDEs.
