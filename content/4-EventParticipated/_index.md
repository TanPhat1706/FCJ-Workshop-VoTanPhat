During the AWS Workforce Bootcamp program, I had the privilege of participating in three major events. Each event not only provided deep technical insights into Cloud technologies, AI, and software architecture, but also offered a fantastic opportunity to network, hone system design thinking, and refine the problem-solving skills of a professional engineer.

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)  
&emsp;**Event Name:** FCAJ Community Day (May)  

&emsp;**Date & Time:** 09:00 - 12:00, May 23, 2026  

&emsp;**Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu St, Dist 1, HCMC  

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee  

&emsp;**Description:** Attended deep-dive sessions on AI applications, Multi-Agent system architecture, Amazon CloudFront, and listened to real-world Hackathon implementation experiences.

&emsp;**Value Gained:** Grasped context-driven AI design principles, understood distributed Multi-Agent architectural models, and learned infrastructure optimization using CloudFront.
