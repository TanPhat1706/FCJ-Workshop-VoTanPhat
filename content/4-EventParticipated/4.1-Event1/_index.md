### Key Takeaways

#### Technical & Architectural Mindset
- **Context-driven AI:** In AI development, building a robust context retrieval system (Memory/Second Brain) is more critical than mere prompt engineering.
- **Cloud-Native Architecture:** Utilizing a CDN like CloudFront goes beyond caching; it serves as a foundational layer for security and cost optimization.
- **Multi-Agent Design:** Breaking down complex tasks (e.g., credit scoring) into autonomous agents is more scalable and secure than relying on a single, overloaded agent.

#### Practical Experience
- Gained a deep understanding of LLM mechanics at the inference layer, enabling the design of fault-tolerant systems that account for non-deterministic outputs.
- Learned agile problem-solving and crisis management through the 36-hour hackathon case study.

### Applying to Work

- **Optimize AI integrations:** Implement better context management (memory) for any chatbot or AI-driven backend services currently in development.
- **Leverage Amazon CloudFront:** Configure CloudFront to distribute content and APIs to offload backend servers (like Spring Boot/NodeJS) while enhancing security.
- **Explore Multi-Agent Models:** Experiment with coordinating multiple AI Agents for complex data processing tasks rather than relying on one monolithic model.
- **Implement LLM Guardrails:** Establish verification mechanisms when integrating LLM APIs, especially for business logic requiring high accuracy, to mitigate non-determinism risks.
