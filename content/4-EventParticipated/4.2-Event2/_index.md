### Event Objectives

- Comprehensively review and solidify AWS cloud knowledge through a gamified experience.
- Enhance teamwork, risk management, and strategic decision-making skills under time pressure.
- Provide a networking and recreational space, allowing interns to balance intensive learning with engaging extracurricular activities.
- Assess system design capabilities ranging from foundational to expert levels (Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect Associate - SAA, Solutions Architect Professional - SAP).

### Competition Structure & Rules

The competition featured **8 teams** battling through multiple rounds of questions. Each round consisted of 10 questions with point values reflecting their difficulty:
- **Cloud Practitioner:** 10 points/question
- **SAA (Solutions Architect Associate):** 20 points/question
- **SAP (Solutions Architect Professional):** 50 points/question

#### Strategic Power-Ups
Each team was provided with 2 tactical skills to alter the scoring dynamics:
1. **Hope Star (High Risk - High Reward):** A correct answer doubles the points earned, but an incorrect answer results in a deduction of double the points.
2. **Risk Mitigation (Safe Play):** A correct answer yields only half (50%) of the points, but an incorrect answer incurs no point deduction.

#### Tournament Stages
- **Qualifying Round (10 questions):** 7 Cloud Practitioner, 2 SAA, 1 SAP.
- **Semi-Final (10 questions):** 5 Cloud Practitioner, 3 SAA, 2 SAP.
- **Final (10 questions):** 3 Cloud Practitioner, 4 SAA, 3 SAP.

### Key Takeaways

#### Strategic Thinking and Risk Management
Deciding when to use the "Hope Star" versus "Risk Mitigation" closely mirrors real-world software architecture decisions. There are scenarios where accepting risk is necessary for maximum performance (rapid scaling), and others where absolute safety is required (zero downtime/no penalty) even if the immediate reward is lower. This honed my ability to evaluate trade-offs—a vital skill for any Senior Developer or Solutions Architect.

#### Cloud Architecture Foundations
By tackling SAA and SAP standard questions, I identified critical blind spots in my knowledge regarding AWS security, networking, and cost optimization. The sheer volume of architectural patterns encountered during the competition strengthened my holistic view of designing distributed applications.

### Applying to Work

- **Fail-Fast & Safe Deployment Mindset:** Inspired by the "Risk Mitigation" strategy, I am applying safer deployment strategies to existing CI/CD pipelines (for NodeJS/Spring Boot projects). Releasing features in smaller, controlled phases helps mitigate the risk of catastrophic impacts on our PostgreSQL databases.
- **System Architecture Upgrade:** Leveraging insights from the SAA/SAP questions to audit current project architectures. I plan to evaluate whether our systems truly achieve High Availability and if our database designs are optimized for cloud I/O performance.

### Event Experience

The June 20th competition at the AWS office brought a refreshing dynamic to the internship journey. Instead of traditional technical seminars, turning knowledge into a live arena made the learning process highly engaging.

Although our team was eliminated in the very first qualifying round, it was an incredibly fun and rewarding "failure."
- Firstly, grappling with SAA and SAP questions right out of the gate made me realize the vastness of the cloud computing landscape and highlighted areas where I need further study.
- Secondly, the event perfectly balanced "education" with "entertainment." Competing alongside colleagues in the modern, dynamic environment of the AWS office relieved stress and significantly boosted team bonding.

Looking back, being "eliminated early" actually afforded me the opportunity to sit back and observe the strategies of the advancing teams. I learned how they analyzed problems under pressure and absorbed advanced architectural concepts discussed in the Semi-Finals and Finals.
